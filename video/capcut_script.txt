Manhole Pilgrim 蓋めぐり — 演示视频旁白稿（英文配音）
用法 A：剪映 → 导入 narration.srt 作为字幕 → 全选字幕 → 文本朗读，选英文男声。
用法 B：逐段复制下面的文字到剪映「文本朗读」。段落之间留约 0.5 秒停顿。
预计总时长：150 秒

[s01 · intro-cover · 6.4s]
Japan has fifteen million manhole covers. Thousands are works of art, cast in iron by the town they belong to.

[s02 · intro-card · 7.4s]
Since 2016, sewer agencies have turned them into free manhole cards, each with the cover, its coordinates, and its story.

[s03 · intro-rule · 6.5s]
One rule: you can't buy them or order them online. You walk up to one specific counter, in one town, and ask.

[s04 · intro-why · 8.3s]
That makes them perfect for travellers: a free souvenir, and a reason to step off the tourist trail. Many counters are tourist offices you'd pass anyway.

[s05 · intro-problem · 5.6s]
But the official list is forty-seven pages of Japanese addresses, with no map. So we built one.

[s06 · title · 3.7s]
Manhole Pilgrim. Every card in Japan, on one map.

[s07 · map-overview · 8.1s]
We geocoded all thirteen hundred counters with Amazon Location Places: Geocode for addresses, Search Text for places with only a name.

[s08 · timeline · 8.2s]
Ten years of releases. Series one had thirty cards. Every few months a new series lands, until all forty-seven prefectures are covered.

[s09 · popup · 5.7s]
Click any card to see its design, where to pick it up, and the opening hours, with a link to the official page.

[s10 · sewer-filter · 8.7s]
Filter by where you pick them up. One card in five is only handed out at a sewage or water plant. The cards were built to bring people into infrastructure they never see.

[s11 · plan-kyoto-iso · 10.2s]
Now the traveller's question: I'm in Kyoto this weekend, how many can I collect? Calculate Isolines draws the real one-hour drive area. About a hundred cards fall inside.

[s12 · plan-kyoto-route · 7.6s]
Optimize Waypoints turns the six nearest into a loop with arrival times. That is an easy afternoon, and six cards you can only get here.

[s13 · plan-sapporo · 8.4s]
Some starts, like the middle of Sapporo Station, can't be driven back to. Then the app falls back to a route matrix and plans a one-way trip on real drive times.

[s14 · disc-lonely · 6.6s]
The map also surprised us. The loneliest card is on the Ogasawara Islands, nine hundred kilometres from any other.

[s15 · disc-border · 8.0s]
And this Hyogo card can only be collected in Osaka. Our geocoder flagged it as an error. It wasn't. The treatment plant sits across the border.

[s16 · disc-roadside · 5.9s]
Ninety-eight cards wait at michi-no-eki roadside stations. Manhole cards are a road-trip game.

[s17 · arch · 17.1s]
How it works: scrape the registry, geocode with Amazon Location, and merge into an Apache Iceberg table on S3 with Athena. The site is static on Amplify. The browser key can only load map tiles. Every paid call goes through a rate-limited Lambda proxy with a daily cap.

[s18 · iceberg · 6.1s]
Each series is an Iceberg snapshot you can time-travel to. Swap the scraper, and it works for any stamp rally.

[s19 · outro · 2.4s]
Manhole Pilgrim. Go find the sewers.
