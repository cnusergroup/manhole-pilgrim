      About Manhole Pilgrim

      An interactive map of all 1,313 Japanese manhole cards (マンホールカード), series 1 to 29, issued from April 2016 to July 2026 across all 47 prefectures. Manhole cards are free collectible cards that you can only get in person, at one specific counter per card.

      Dataset: the official GKP (下水道広報プラットホーム) registry at gk-p.jp/mhcard, with 47 prefecture pages of free-text Japanese addresses and no coordinates. Every distribution counter was geocoded with Amazon Location Service Places (Geocode, and SearchText for name-only places): 1,304 of 1,313 to a street address or point of interest.

      What you can explore: filter by series, prefecture and the type of place that hands the card out (265 at sewage or water plants, 98 at michi-no-eki roadside stations), and replay ten years of releases on a timeline. Pick a start point and a drive time to see the cards inside an Amazon Location CalculateIsolines drive-time area, and get an OptimizeWaypoints pilgrimage route with arrival times.

      AWS services: Amazon Location Service (Maps, Places, Routes); Apache Iceberg tables on Amazon S3 via Amazon Athena and AWS Glue (one snapshot per series, time travel); AWS Amplify Hosting; Amazon API Gateway, AWS Lambda and Amazon DynamoDB as a rate-limited proxy with daily caps; Amazon Polly for the demo narration.
